Moving to Halifax with a child: which numbers can you use?

28 November 2026

A family looking for a two-bedroom apartment and infant care in Halifax faces two different searches. The rent figure depends on whose rent is being measured. The child-care count depends on the age a provider actually serves. Here is how to narrow both using the records available on November 28, 2026.

Which rent belongs in a moving budget?

In the second quarter of 2026, a two-bedroom apartment in the Halifax census metropolitan area averaged $2,400 a month in advertised asking rent. The average paid rent for an occupied two-bedroom was $1,770. A household using the paid average to budget for a new listing would start $630 a month below the asking average. The listings and occupied homes can differ in location, amenities and lease age. Use actual listings for the neighbourhood you are considering.

Open the Halifax rent history, then compare the two-bedroom rows for the same quarter. The figures are experimental estimates in Statistics Canada table 46-10-0092-01. The survey methodology explains which homes each estimate covers.

Which providers actually list infant care?

The June 30, 2026 edition of the Nova Scotia directory has 73 licensed facility records with Halifax in the city field. Of those, 60 flag infant or toddler care and 26 explicitly flag infant care. All 26 also flag a full-day program at the facility. The separate age and program fields tell you whom to contact. Confirm the hours for your child’s age, the start date, fees and wait-list process with each provider.

Open the Halifax infant-care contact list to shortlist providers and check their addresses. You can reproduce the infant count using the province’s filtered data and read coverage dates in the source directory. This edition records licensed services. Openings for a particular date require a call or email.

Match the search to your commute

The rent estimate covers the metropolitan area. The 73 child-care records use the literal locality “Halifax.” Dartmouth and Bedford have their own directory entries within the metro area. If a listing across the harbour suits your budget, search its locality too before judging how many providers are within reach.
