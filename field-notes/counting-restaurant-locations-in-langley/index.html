How many restaurant locations are there in Langley?

27 November 2026

302 in Langley Township and 61 in Langley City. Both figures come from Statistics Canada’s June 2026 count of full-service restaurants and limited-service eating places with employees, retrieved November 27, 2026. The two rows carry the same place name in the download. Their geographic identifiers distinguish the municipalities. Open the two Langley records.

What goes into the count?

Choose industry group 7225 and employment size Total, with employees in Statistics Canada table 33-10-1176-01. It counts operating locations, so a chain can contribute several sites. A business with no employees falls outside this table. To look up individual establishments in the Township, search the municipal licence records in Langley Trades. The licence file has its own coverage and observation date.

Try the same question in Halifax

Under the same industry and employee settings, Halifax Regional Municipality has 915 locations. The wider Halifax metropolitan area has 935. The two Halifax boundaries overlap. Adding those counts together would count municipal locations twice. Compare the Halifax boundaries.

What do the businesses spend?

ISED’s financial report for restaurant group 7225 covers Canadian businesses with annual revenues between $30,000 and $5 million. In the 2024 edition, the 65,071 businesses in that revenue range averaged $849,200 in revenue and $827,700 in expenses. Business Bench displays ISED’s reliability indicator beside each average. Multiplying the national average revenue by 302 would mix businesses and locations, different years and different populations. Compare your own expenses with restaurant peers in Business Bench.
