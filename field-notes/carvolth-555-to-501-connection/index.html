How much time do you need to change from the 555 to the 501 at Carvolth?

28 November 2026

The eastbound 555 from Lougheed drops passengers at Carvolth Exchange’s Bay 1. The 501 to Langley Centre leaves from Bay 3. On the Monday, November 30, 2026 timetable, the 4:24 p.m. arrival gave you seven minutes to catch the 4:31 p.m. departure. Allow five minutes to change bays and only two minutes of that gap remain. Try the same connection on the next weekday in the current timetable.

What happens if the 555 arrives five minutes late?

At 4:29 p.m., the same five-minute walk gets you to Bay 3 at 4:34. The next scheduled 501 is at 4:46. That shifts your onward departure by 15 minutes. The 4:36 p.m. 555 arrival illustrates a different outcome: a five-minute delay and a five-minute walk put you at the bay at 4:46, exactly on the departure time. Transfer Window counts that as reachable, with no spare minute.

Across all six scheduled 555 arrivals between 4 and 5 p.m. that day, four kept their original 501 departure under this delay scenario. Two shifted by 15 minutes: the 4:24 and 4:53 arrivals. The calculation assumes the onward 501 leaves on schedule. The five-minute walk is an input you can change for your pace, a stroller or mobility aid.

How busy is the eastbound 555 in the afternoon?

In TransLink’s fall 2025 weekday data, the 555’s afternoon average peak load factor was 95.7% eastbound towards Carvolth and 44.7% westbound towards Lougheed. The measure averages the load at the busiest point of each trip as a percentage of vehicle capacity. It gives a sense of the difference between directions. It cannot tell you how many people will get off at Bay 1 on a particular trip. Read the 555 route history or compare the fall 2025 weekday directions.
