How early did RV campers book July weekends at Rissers Beach and Graves Island?

28 November 2026

For RV campers arriving on a Friday or Saturday in July, the typical reservation was made 95 days ahead at Rissers Beach and 46 days ahead at Graves Island. That seven-week difference comes from comparing the same equipment and arrival days at two Nova Scotia South Shore parks over 2011–2024.

How common was it to book three months ahead?

At Rissers Beach, 1,749 of the 2,974 qualifying July weekend RV reservations were made at least 91 days ahead (58.8%). At Graves Island, 1,225 of 3,240 were made that early (37.8%). If your park and arrival day are fixed, the Rissers pattern gives you a reason to check the provincial booking opening early. You can open both weekend distributions in Camp Calendar, including reservations made during the last week.

What if you can arrive on Sunday?

Campers arriving Sunday through Thursday booked a median of 74 days ahead at Rissers (6,325 reservations) and 17 days ahead at Graves (5,006). At Graves, moving from the Friday/Saturday group to the Sunday–Thursday group changes the historical median by 29 days. Compare the two parks for Sunday–Thursday arrivals.

If you can switch parks or change your arrival date, check both calendars for your actual stay. The RV filter groups reservations by equipment. If you need hookups, check the serviced sites and maps for Rissers Beach or Graves Island. On November 28, 2026, Nova Scotia Parks listed April 2027 as the opening month for 2027 campsite reservations. Check its reservation page for the coming season.

What did the province actually record?

These figures use overnight campsites in the provincial reservation archive, as checked on November 28, 2026. Each lead time is the number of days from reservation creation to arrival. The median is the middle of the recorded stays when sorted by lead time. The weekday group includes Sundays, and the grouping uses the arrival day regardless of how long a trip lasted.

The archive covers successful bookings across fourteen arrival years. It cannot tell us when a park sold out, how many people searched unsuccessfully, or whether a site is free now. To explore tent bookings or other months, start with the Rissers Beach July guide or Graves Island July guide.
