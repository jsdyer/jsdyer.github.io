Can rent outweigh the nursing tuition difference between Nova Scotia and B.C.?

28 November 2026

$510.75 a month is the break-even point for an eight-month lease. That is how much more a student could spend on rent in B.C. before using up the province’s $4,086 average nursing tuition advantage for 2026/2027. The result changes if you share a place or have to sign for the full year.

Start with one year of nursing tuition

Statistics Canada’s preliminary 2026/2027 average for full-time Canadian undergraduate nursing students is $9,803 in Nova Scotia and $5,717 in British Columbia. These are enrolment-weighted provincial averages in Canadian dollars. In Nova Scotia, the average combines fees paid by in-province and out-of-province Canadian students. Check the amount your school charges for your program and residency status. Additional compulsory fees and living costs belong on separate lines in a budget.

Compare the Nova Scotia nursing tuition history and the British Columbia history. The published figures come from Statistics Canada’s undergraduate tuition table and its survey of full-time degree programs.

What changes if you share a two-bedroom?

In April through June 2026, the average advertised rent for an entire two-bedroom apartment was $2,400 a month in metro Halifax and $3,030 in metro Vancouver. Those are experimental estimates for listings in each metropolitan area. They give a benchmark while you look for an actual room or apartment near campus.

Suppose you and one roommate split rent evenly and pay for eight months in either city. Your share would be $1,200 in Halifax and $1,515 in Vancouver. That extra $315 a month in Vancouver adds up to $2,520. Adding eight months of your share to the published provincial tuition gives $19,403 in Halifax and $17,837 in Vancouver. Vancouver comes out $1,566 lower in this example.

Pay for the whole two-bedroom yourself in both cities and the answer reverses. The full $630 monthly rent difference adds up to $5,040 in eight months, exceeding the $4,086 tuition difference. Halifax comes out $954 lower.

See the unit sizes and quarterly histories in the Halifax rent report and Vancouver rent report. To reproduce the two-bedroom figures, select “Average asking rent” in Statistics Canada’s quarterly rent table.

Use the months you will actually pay for

Take the difference between your two tuition quotes and divide it by the number of months you will pay rent in both places. Over 12 months, the same $4,086 gap works out to $340.50 per month. If the leases have different lengths, calculate tuition plus your monthly share times the lease length for each offer separately. Then add your own fees, utilities and travel to clinical placements before comparing the totals.
