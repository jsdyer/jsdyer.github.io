# Canadian geographic coverage review

On November 28, 2026 we screened the descriptions of all 295 tools in the [catalogue](../../data/tools.json). We inspected the shortlisted tools’ actual inputs to distinguish an interactive search’s reach from the places with saved history. The following list records what could usefully expand and what a future release still needs to verify.

## First release

| Tool | Added coverage | What a reader can do |
|---|---|---|
| [Care Compass](https://coolcode.ca/carecompass/) | New Brunswick joins British Columbia and Nova Scotia | Find providers by community and language of service, check licensed age groups and build a shortlist. The [facility directory](https://gnb.socrata.com/d/q6ky-jc7a) supplies one record per licence. The [sites file](https://gnb.socrata.com/d/46xa-wxhc) supplies public contact and location details for unambiguous matches. |
| [Pantry Prices](https://coolcode.ca/pantryprices/#coffee) | The coffee comparison expands from B.C. and Nova Scotia to all ten provinces | Compare the same 340 g package in two provinces or track each price against the same Arabica benchmark. Statistics Canada supplies the retail series. [Monthly retail prices](https://doi.org/10.25318/1810024501-eng) |
| [Library Workbench](https://coolcode.ca/libraryworkbench/#print-planner) | Adds the Oakville and Toronto public library systems to the Halifax and Richmond print planner | Enter print time and filament weight, compare published fees and booking limits, and check an individual library’s policy. [Oakville](https://opl.ca/library-services/technology/3d-printing) · [Toronto](https://tpl.ca/using-the-library/computer-services/digital-innovation-services/3d-printers/) |

## Where local scope belongs

- Keep [Camp Calendar](https://coolcode.ca/campcalendar/) focused on Nova Scotia provincial park reservations, [Halifax Time Lens](https://coolcode.ca/halifaxtimelens/) on its matched local imagery, and [Transfer Window](https://coolcode.ca/transferwindow/) on scheduled route 555 connections at Carvolth and Lougheed. Different cities have different transfer pairs, walking times and service calendars. [Camp Calendar source](https://data.novascotia.ca/datasets/4zt7-x443) · [Transfer Window methodology](https://github.com/jsdyer/transferwindow)
- [Park Visits](https://coolcode.ca/parkvisits/) covers Parks Canada sites nationally despite opening with Fort Langley and Halifax Citadel. [Price Atlas](https://coolcode.ca/priceatlas/) already covers all ten provinces. Whitehorse and Yellowknife are city series, and the cited monthly table has no comparable Nunavut series. A territory-wide grocery comparison remains blocked by source coverage. [Price Atlas coverage notes](https://github.com/jsdyer/priceatlas#geographic-coverage)
- [Spill Ledger](https://coolcode.ca/spillledger/) covers the specific federal compensation cases in the reviewed report table. To add a province, first identify comparable incident and claimant rows with verified financial stages in another section or edition. A missing province in this extract says nothing about how often spills occur there. [Reviewed claims appendix](https://ship-rail.gc.ca/sites/default/files/2025-09/ship_fund_-_2023-2024_annual_report.pdf#page=63)

## U.S. coverage

After the Canadian pilots, Sun Ledger and Flight Weather are natural U.S. candidates because the same publishers expose the same variables and units on both sides of the border. A city such as Winnipeg and a nearby U.S. city could share a solar baseline. For price, licensing or regulatory tools, a U.S. series would warrant its own definitions, currency and jurisdiction labels before offering cross-border comparisons.
