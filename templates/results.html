Explore by place

Halifax and Dartmouth

Read the Lake Banook water measurements, check Halifax employment estimates or compare donair counter prices and clams and chips menus. Explore the fisheries data in the Nova Scotia clam landings series.

Trace Dartmouth’s post office from a shop at King and Portland to its 1894 brick building. Compare later archive views of the ferry waterfront. On the coast, the Peggy’s Point light record gives the duration of its red flash.

See how Bedford Basin’s water level differed from the predicted tide during Dorian in 2019 and Fiona in 2022.

Metro Vancouver and Langley

Look up Fort Langley visitor counts, Vancouver labour market figures or SeaBus departures from Waterfront. Compare eastbound and westbound passenger loads on route 555 during the morning and afternoon peaks. For the Bowen Island crossing, see the Horseshoe Bay–Snug Cove passenger history.

Compare the Grouse Mountain snow survey with earlier winters, or read the reported attendance at Port Kells school alongside the province’s scanned report.

Follow the Yorkson Creek coho record or see the annual crash counts at Douglas Crescent and 203 Street around the road project.

Chilliwack River watershed
